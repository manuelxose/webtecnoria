# Competitor analysis

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Method:** public web search results and competitor pages, plus repository evidence. `[SERP]` below
means a page was observed for a query; it does **not** mean a rank position or local-pack presence.
No volumes, difficulty scores or traffic estimates were available and none are invented.

## 1. Galicia / Vigo set

| Player | Observed positioning | What it means for Tecnoria |
|---|---|---|
| **Q2BSTUDIO** | RPA + IA + software; multiple near-duplicate local URLs for “automatización de procesos en Vigo” | Local demand exists, but the templated city-page pattern is a doorway risk Tecnoria must not copy. |
| **BlackHold Consulting** | “Presencia especial en Vigo y Galicia”, AI agents with company data | Direct clash on “IA + Galicia”; Tecnoria needs a real locality story or an explicit remote-first statement. |
| **Medranet** | Vigo cooperative; AI agents/chatbots/RAG + technical SEO/GEO/AEO + custom web | Same three offers plus SEO; likely strong on AI + conversion terms. |
| **Morse / Possible / Inovalabs** | AI on-premise, DevOps, AI Act; consulting/software/mobile/security; automation + BI/IA | Sector associations already index them; Tecnoria is absent from those directories. |
| **ITG** | Free digital diagnostics for Galician firms | A free “diagnóstico” competes with paid discovery; the paid-vs-free story must be explicit. |
| **Cluster TIC Galicia / Gaiástech** | Public supplier maps by service and province | Highest-value local citation targets; membership/eligibility must be confirmed, not assumed. |

## 2. National reference set

- **Kōdo Factory**: software factory + AI agents, productised entry price, “prototipo 48 h”, “MVP
  2–4 semanas”, IA360/Kit Digital content, anti-vendor buyer guide, own product. Closest analogue:
  sells a method and a price anchor, not a stack.
- **Indicus**: custom software + API integrations + “IA donde aporta valor real”.
- **Sysprovider**: web applications, portals/SaaS, Spanish hosting, 24×7 infrastructure trust.
- **Fides / Base10**: consultancy bundles with IA and managed services.

## 3. Defensible differentiation

1. Tecnoria ships and operates **its own SaaS** (Talkaris, Auctorio) and a live client platform
   (Guía de Programación TV) with SSR/SEO. No Galicia competitor observed shows comparable product
   work.
2. Full chain under one accountable team: discovery → build → automation → AI → evolution.
3. Concrete engineering substance already in the repo: multi-tenant isolation, dry-run publishing,
   escalation rules, QA gates. Publish that instead of adjectives.

## 4. Competitive gaps Tecnoria can own

- A real **engagement model** with phases and a price anchor (after owner decision).
- **Decision content**: build vs buy, how to evaluate an AI supplier, when not to automate.
- **Sector proof** in industria/distribución, servicios B2B and media, only where a client permits it.
- **AI governance for pymes** (AI Act, human review, evaluation) explained in plain Spanish.
- **Local visibility** only after the legal entity and locality are resolved.

## 5. What was not measured

No keyword volumes, keyword difficulty, backlink gap, traffic estimates or local-pack positions. A
real competitor gap analysis requires Search Console (for Tecnoria) and a keyword/backlink dataset.
Those are recorded as access blockers, not filled with invented numbers.
