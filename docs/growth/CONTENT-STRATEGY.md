# Content strategy

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Scope:** public site content, not a publishing schedule with invented volumes.
**Evidence base:** `docs/growth/_team/GROWTH-STRATEGY.md`, repository content, live pages.

## 1. What is already strong

- Six service landings with distinct problem/solution/process/FAQ sections.
- 13 static articles across build, automation, chatbots, AI, legacy and operations.
- Two own products with dedicated pages and a named client platform case.
- One clear conversion path: service/article → contact form.

The gap is not “more words”. It is **specific proof**, **decision-stage content** and a **verified
business identity**.

## 2. Content that must not be published

- Anonymous testimonials presented as proof.
- Generic “case studies” without a named client or permission.
- Precise metrics (60%, 99.9%, <200 ms, 10×) without a stated method, sample and period.
- City × service pages with swapped city names.
- Scraped-question FAQ pages.
- Kit Digital/IA360 pages that imply accreditation without evidence.
- Machine-translated Galician pages without a human review process.

## 4. Editorial standard

Every new page must have:

1. A specific reader and problem in the first 100 words.
2. A differentiated answer that a sibling page does not already own.
3. A named author/company responsibility path (no anonymous expertise).
4. A source or method for every number.
5. A visible next step and links to 2–3 relevant service/article pages.
6. A review date and owner.
7. A place in the sitemap and at least one contextual internal link.

## 6. Measurement per asset

Track non-brand impressions and clicks by cluster, then form starts and submitted leads. Do not
judge a page by sessions alone. A page with impressions but low CTR is a title/description problem;
a page with clicks but no leads is a conversion or intent-match problem.
