## 1. Offer actually sold

Six service lines (`site-content.ts:526-1362`): software a medida, automatización de procesos,
chatbots, IA aplicada, plataformas/SaaS and consultoría tecnológica. Delivery is phased:
Discovery → Blueprint → Build → Launch → Evolution (`site-content.ts:392-423`). Two own products
(Talkaris, Auctorio) and one named client platform (Guía de Programación TV) are the strongest
proof assets.

## 3. Cannibalisation controls

1. **Chatbot demand is split three ways** (service page, Talkaris product page, article). Enforce
   different titles/H1/FAQ: service = implementation, product = platform, article = decision.
2. **Software a medida** appears on the landing, SaaS landing and two articles. Each must keep a
   distinct angle: custom build, product build, cost and build-vs-buy.
3. **Discovery/consultoría** overlaps the consulting landing, `/metodologia` and the discovery
   article. Keep `/metodologia` about process, the landing about buying the service.
4. **FAQPage** is repeated on home, `/faq` and every service page. Google retired FAQ rich results
   in May 2026; keep the visible Q&A, do not expect a rich result and do not add more FAQ-only pages.

## 5. Programmatic SEO policy

Allowed only when every page has first-party evidence: case-study pages with real problem, solution
and measured impact. Forbidden: city × service matrices, sector × service templates, scraped-question
pages and any page whose only difference is a swapped noun. A page must pass all six gates:
first-party data, a unique human-written paragraph, unique title/H1, hub links, sitemap inclusion,
owner and review date.

## 6. Scoring model

Score each opportunity 1–5 on: relevance to the six services; commercial intent; evidence of demand
(GSC/SERP, never invented); competition; existing ranking opportunity (GSC position 8–20 first);
implementation cost; expected business value. Do not publish a number without the source and date.

## 7. Decisions blocked on the owner

Real publishable address/locality; legal name and NIF; which clients may be named and which metrics
may be published; pricing policy; Kit Digital/IA360 accreditation; coverage (Galicia, Spain or both);
languages (Spanish only or Spanish + Galician). Until these are answered, no local page, pricing page
or case-study detail page should be published.
